## Service: Accounting & Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping is one of Effinance's three core service pillars, covering the systematic recording of all major and minor business transactions. Outsourcing this allows business owners to reclaim time for growth instead of manual record-keeping.

**Bookkeeping tasks include:**
- Reviewing chart of accounts
- Reclassifying transactions into appropriate categories
- Maintaining consistency for accrual-basis clients and year-end financial adjustments

**Three types of Bookkeeping Services offered:**
1. **General Bookkeeping Services** — clients provide statement details; Effinance uploads entries into the client's preferred accounting software.
2. **CPA Write-Up Services** — full maintenance of ledgers, accounts receivable/payable entries, and account reconciliation, ensuring every transaction is transparent and clear.
3. **Reports** — including monthly/quarterly trial balances, ledger entries, and debtor/creditor reports prioritized by aging.

Bookkeeping services can be outsourced fully or partially depending on the client's needs and budget.

## Sub-Service: Cash Flow Forecasting

Cash flow forecasting helps a business understand its budget relative to income generation and required expenses, flagging potential shortfalls or surpluses in advance so funds can be redirected or invested wisely.

**Cash Flow Chart categories:**
- **Cash from Operations** — Inflow: income from core business operations (buying/selling). Outflow: inventory purchases, taxes, and miscellaneous operating expenses.
- **Cash from Investments** — Inflow: proceeds from selling large investments (fixed assets, property). Outflow: cash spent on new investments or replacing old assets.
- **Cash from Financing** — Inflow: cash raised from shares or loans. Outflow: cash spent on loan repayments.
